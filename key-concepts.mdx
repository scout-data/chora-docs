Chora has two sides: a **hiring team** on one side, and the **scouts** they invite on the other. Everything below exists to connect those two sides around a hire.

## The two sides

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Hiring team" icon="building">
    The company doing the hiring. Posts roles, invites scouts, confirms hires, pays the scout fee. Works out of `/hiring`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scout" icon="user-plus">
    An individual with a network, referring candidates. Shares role links, tracks their pipeline, gets paid when a hire lands. Works out of `/scouts`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Core objects

**Programme.** A hiring team's setup on Chora: the company name, logo, scouts, roles, and payout config, all bundled. One company = one programme. Scouts join programmes; they don't join Chora at large.

**Role.** An open job the hiring team wants to fill. Scouts can only refer candidates to roles with status **Active**. Closed roles still appear in referral history but can't accept new ones.

**Share link.** A personal, trackable URL the scout copies from the Roles tab and sends to someone. Looks like `app.trychora.com/go/<role-slug>?ref=<scout-token>`. Every click, application, interview, and hire that comes through this link is attributed back to the scout who shared it.

**Referral.** A candidate who's touched a scout's share link. Starts life as "Shared" and progresses through pipeline stages until it either lands as a Hire or ends as Rejected/Withdrawn.

**Invite link.** Separate thing. This is what a hiring team sends to a *scout* so they can join the programme. Not to be confused with a share link.

## Scout classes

When a scout signs up, Chora classifies them automatically:

| Class | How it's decided | How they're paid |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | Their email matches a domain the hiring team has verified (e.g. `@acme.com`) | Via the hiring team's payroll. Chora exports a CSV, the team runs payroll |
| **External** | Any other email | Via Stripe Connect. Scout adds their own bank details; Chora pays direct |

Classification happens once at sign-up but can be changed by the hiring admin.

## Pipeline stages

A referral moves through these states, in order, as the hiring team progresses or rejects the candidate:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shared">
    Scout has shared the link. No one's clicked yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clicked">
    A candidate has opened the link. No application yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Applied">
    The candidate submitted the role's application form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interviewing">
    The hiring team is actively progressing them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Offer out">
    The team has made an offer. Awaiting candidate's decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hired">
    Candidate accepted. Scout fee is quoted but not yet paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmed">
    The hiring team has confirmed the hire. Chora charges the hirer's card and queues the scout payout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two terminal states exist off the happy path:

- **Rejected.** The hiring team isn't moving forward with the candidate.
- **Withdrawn.** The candidate pulled out themselves.

Neither triggers a payout.

## Money terms

**Scout fee.** The flat GBP amount the scout receives when their referral is confirmed as a hire. Set programme-wide as a default, or overridden per role. Quoted upfront to the scout. Once a scout sees the fee on a role, that's the number they get.

**Chora fee.** Platform fee the hiring team pays on top of the scout fee. **20% on the Free plan, 15% on Pro.** Charged alongside the scout fee the moment the hire is confirmed.

**Hirer total.** Scout fee + Chora fee. The amount actually charged to the hiring team's card. A £500 scout fee on Free plan = £600 total charge.

**Payout holding period.** Optional buffer the hiring team can set between confirming a hire and releasing the scout fee. Useful as a safety net against cancelled offers or candidate no-shows. Defaults to 0 days (instant release).

**Payout states**, as seen on `/scouts/payouts`:

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Hire confirmed, scout fee locked, waiting for Stripe to pick up the transfer |
| **In transit** | Stripe has the transfer but hasn't settled it to the bank yet |
| **Completed** | Money is in the scout's bank account |
| **Failed** | Transfer bounced. Usually bank details need updating |

The [**Referrals tab**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/referrals) is your pipeline view. Every candidate who's touched one of your links appears here with a live status.

## The seven stages

| Stage | Means |
|---|---|
| **Shared** | You've shared the link; nobody's clicked yet |
| **Clicked** | A candidate opened your link |
| **Applied** | A candidate submitted the application |
| **Interviewing** | The hiring team is actively progressing them |
| **Offer out** | They've been offered the role |
| **Hired** | They accepted and the hire is confirmed |
| **Rejected / Withdrawn** | Pipeline ended, either rejected or candidate withdrew |

Clicking any row opens a side drawer with the full timeline: when you shared it, when they clicked, every stage transition, and the fee you'll earn if it lands.

## Overview page

Your [**Overview**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts) shows the at-a-glance version:

- **Earnings chart.** Cumulative scout fees over 7, 30, 90 days, or all-time
- **Top programmes.** Which companies have earned you the most
- **Recent referrals.** Last five with live stage
- **Top roles.** Which roles have paid out best

Pick the time range from the dropdown in the top-right of the page.

## Email updates

Chora emails you when a referral changes stage. You control which events you want notifications for at [**/scouts/notifications**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/notifications). See [Email notifications](/scouts/email-notifications).

## Private vs public visibility

Only you and the hiring admin can see your referrals. Other scouts on the same programme can't see who you've referred or what they're doing.

## When to nudge

The platform doesn't tell you to nudge, but if a candidate's been in **Interviewing** for more than 7 days without moving, a short message from you (a tip, a question, encouragement) often tips the decision. The person hired is the same person you referred; they trust you more than the recruiter.

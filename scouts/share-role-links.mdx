title: Share role links
description: Copy a role's link, send it anywhere, and get credited for every click and hire.
---

Every role on Chora has a **personal share link** for you. Links track clicks, candidate submissions, interviews, and hires, all attributed back to you automatically.

## Copy a link

1. Go to [**/scouts/roles**](https://trychora.com/scouts/roles).
2. Find the role you want to share.
3. Click the **Links** column. Chora copies the URL to your clipboard.

The link looks like `trychora.com/go/<slug>?ref=<your-token>`. The token identifies you; the slug identifies the role.

<Tip>
  You can share the same link in multiple places. Chora dedupes candidates. If the same person opens it from Slack and WhatsApp and then applies, you're credited once.
</Tip>

## Where to share

| Channel | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Direct message** (Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage) | Highest conversion, since you know the person |
| **Email** | Good for past colleagues |
| **LinkedIn DMs** | Discovery mode, lower conversion |
| **Public posts** | Casts widest net but weakest signal |

Chora's data consistently shows **DM shares convert ~10× public posts**. Spend your time on 1:1 outreach to people you actually know.

## What happens after a click

1. **Click.** Candidate lands on the role's apply page (hosted on your programme's domain).
2. **Apply.** If they submit, the referral appears in your [Referrals tab](/scouts/track-referrals) as **Shared**.
3. **Interview.** Hiring team moves them along; you get an email.
4. **Hired.** If they land the job, you earn the scout fee.

## Share a programme (not just one role)

If you want to broadcast your whole programme instead of a single role, go to [**/scouts/programmes**](https://trychora.com/scouts/programmes) and copy the programme-level link. Candidates land on the hiring team's careers page with your attribution attached.

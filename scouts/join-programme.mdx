To scout for a company, you need an **invite link** from that company's hiring team. Chora programmes are invite-only.

## Accept the invite

1. Open the invite link. It looks like `app.trychora.com/join/<company>/<token>`.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Chora creates your account and adds you to the programme automatically.

<Note>
  We only use your Google account to confirm your email. No other data is pulled from Google, and we never post on your behalf.
</Note>

## Walk through the quick tour

After signing in, Chora takes you through a four-step walkthrough:

1. **What Chora is.** 60-second explainer
2. **Share a role.** How share links work
3. **Follow your pipeline.** Where to track referrals
4. **Get paid.** Connecting your bank via Stripe

Each step has a **Skip walkthrough** link if you'd rather jump straight to the dashboard.

## Complete your profile

Once you reach [**/scouts**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts), the dashboard shows a **Launch your scout profile** checklist at the top. First item: **Complete your profile**.

Visit [**/scouts/profile**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/profile) and add at least:

- **Name.** Shown on your referrals in the hiring team's view
- **Country.** Pick from the dropdown
- **Avatar.** Upload an image, 2MB max

The rest (bio, website, LinkedIn, contact email) is optional but helps hiring teams recognise you when your referrals come in.

## Scouting for multiple programmes

If you have invite links from several companies, open each one and sign in. Chora merges them into one account. Your dashboard aggregates across every programme by default; your Overview shows totals across all of them.

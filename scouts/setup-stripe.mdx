Chora pays scout fees via **Stripe Connect**. The first time you expect a payout, you'll need to complete a short Stripe onboarding to connect your bank account.

## When to set this up

You can connect Stripe any time, but you only *need* it once a referral is **Hired** and the hiring team has confirmed the hire. Setting it up in advance means the first payout lands faster.

## Connect your bank

1. Go to [**/scouts/payouts**](https://trychora.com/scouts/payouts).
2. Click **Payout settings** in the top-right of the page.
3. Click **Connect bank account**. Opens Stripe's hosted onboarding.
4. Stripe will ask for:
   - Your full legal name
   - Date of birth
   - Address
   - Bank account details (sort code + account number for UK; equivalent for other countries)
5. Click **Submit** on Stripe's side. You bounce back to Chora with the bank preview showing your connected account.

<Note>
  Stripe handles everything to do with your bank. Chora never sees or stores your account number. We only store the connection ID so we can send money to it.
</Note>

## Change your bank account

On [**/scouts/payouts**](https://trychora.com/scouts/payouts), open **Payout settings** and click **Manage on Stripe**. The hosted update flow lets you replace the bank account or update any identity details.

## Internal (employee) scouts

If your sign-up email matches your employer's verified domain, Chora tags you as **Internal** and your scout fees route through your company's payroll. You don't need to connect Stripe. Your Payouts page will say "Paid via payroll" instead of showing a Connect button.

## Supported countries

Stripe Connect supports payouts to 40+ countries. If your country isn't listed during onboarding, email [support@trychora.com](mailto:support@trychora.com) and we'll flag an alternative.

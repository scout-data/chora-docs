---
title: Email notifications
description: Control which scout events reach your inbox.
---

Chora emails you from `notifications@trychora.com` when things happen to your referrals. You can toggle each category on or off at [**/scouts/notifications**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/notifications).

## The four categories

| Category | Fires when |
|---|---|
| **Referral stage updates** | A candidate you referred moves to interviewing, offer out, hired, rejected, or withdrawn |
| **Payout events** | Your scout fee moves to pending, in transit, completed, or failed |
| **New role posted** | A programme you scout for opens a new role |
| **Monthly summary** | 1st of each month. A one-email digest of the previous 30 days |

All four default to **on** when you first join. Flip any toggle and click **Save preferences**.

## Change your email address

By default, Chora sends to the email you signed up with. To route notifications to a different address (personal vs. work), set a **contact email** on your [profile page](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/profile). It overrides the account email for notifications only. Your login still uses the original.

## Unsubscribe from everything

Turn all four toggles off on [**/scouts/notifications**](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/notifications). You'll still receive critical operational emails (payout failures that need your attention, account security) because those aren't optional.

## I'm not getting emails

Check:

1. **Spam / Promotions tab.** Gmail sometimes files `notifications@` senders there. Add `notifications@trychora.com` to your contacts.
2. **Your prefs on [/scouts/notifications](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/notifications).** Make sure the relevant toggle is on.
3. **Your contact email** on [/scouts/profile](https://app.trychora.com/scouts/profile). If it's set to an old or wrong address, notifications go there instead of your account email.

Still nothing? Email [support@trychora.com](mailto:support@trychora.com) with your account email and we'll check the delivery log.

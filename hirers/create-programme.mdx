---
title: Create your programme
description: Set up your company profile so scouts know who they're referring to.
---

Your **programme** is your company's presence on Chora. Scouts see your name, logo, and tagline when they open your invite link and when they share roles.

## Open your programme profile

1. Go to [**/hiring/account**](https://app.trychora.com/hiring/account).
2. Fill in **Company name**, **Website URL**, and upload a **Logo**.
3. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
  Upload a square logo (PNG or SVG) at least 128×128. Chora uses it on your public landing page, in scout dashboards, and inside outbound emails.
</Tip>

## Verify your email domain (optional but recommended)

If employees sign up with an email on your verified domain, Chora automatically tags them as **Internal**. They bypass the external-scout review step and can be paid via payroll rather than Stripe Connect.

1. Scroll to the **Email domains** section on the Account page.
2. Enter your company domain (e.g. `acme.com`) and click **Add**.
3. Add the DNS TXT record Chora displays, then click **Verify**.

## Publish your public landing page

Your public landing page lives at `trychora.com/<your-slug>` and is what scouts see the first time they open an invite link. Visit [**/hiring/public-page**](https://app.trychora.com/hiring/public-page) to customise the hero copy and brand colour, then click **Publish**.

<Note>
  Until you publish, invite links render a generic Chora shell. Publishing is one click and can be re-edited any time.
</Note>

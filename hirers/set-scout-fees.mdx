---
title: Set scout fees
description: Decide how much a scout earns when their referral lands a hire.
---

A **scout fee** is the flat amount Scout pays a scout the moment you confirm one of their referrals has been hired. You set it once as a programme default; you can override it per role.

## Set the programme default

1. Go to [**/hiring/rewards**](https://trychora.com/hiring/rewards).
2. Enter an amount in **Default scout fee** (in GBP).
3. Click **Save**.

Every role you add from now on inherits this amount. Changes don't retroactively adjust fees quoted on referrals that have already been shared. Once a scout sees a fee on a role, that's the amount they get if their person lands the job.

## Per-role overrides

For roles where you want a different fee (senior engineering roles, specialist positions), override the default:

1. Open the role on [**/hiring/roles**](https://trychora.com/hiring/roles).
2. Scroll to **Scout fee** and toggle **Use a custom amount**.
3. Enter the role-specific fee and save.

The scout dashboard surfaces role-specific fees clearly so scouts know which roles pay more.

## How the fee math works

When you confirm a hire:

| Line item | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| **Scout fee (quoted)** | Transferred to the scout in full |
| **Platform fee** | 20% (Free plan) or 15% (Pro plan) on top of the scout fee |
| **Hirer total** | Scout fee + Platform fee, charged to your card |

<Note>
  The Platform fee is on top of the scout fee, not deducted from it. A £500 scout fee on Free plan costs you £600 total (£500 to the scout + £100 to Scout).
</Note>

## Payout timing

Payouts leave Scout the moment you confirm the hire. You can set a **holding period** (e.g. 30 days after confirmation) if you want a buffer against cancelled offers or candidate no-shows. Configure it on [**/hiring/referrals**](https://trychora.com/hiring/referrals) under **Payout settings**.

---
title: Payouts and billing
description: Attach a card so scout fees can settle when you confirm a hire.
---

Chora charges your card the moment you confirm a hire. Until you add a payment method, hires stay in a **pending-charge** state. The scout sees "waiting on hirer" and no money moves.

## Add a payment method

1. Go to [**/hiring/billing**](https://app.trychora.com/hiring/billing).
2. Click **Add payment method**. Opens Stripe's hosted card form.
3. Save. Returns you to the Billing page with your card on file.

You can replace or remove the card any time via **Manage billing** (Stripe's hosted Customer Portal).

## Confirming hires

The scout fee + Chora platform fee are charged when you move a referral to **Hired → Confirmed**:

1. Open [**/hiring/referrals**](https://app.trychora.com/hiring/referrals).
2. Click a referral in the **Hired** stage to open the side drawer.
3. Click **Confirm hire**.
4. Chora charges your card and queues the scout payout.

## Where scouts are paid

| Scout class | Payout route |
|---|---|
| **External** scout | Stripe Connect transfer to their bank |
| **Internal** scout (employee) | Payroll CSV export you send to your payroll provider |

Scouts set up their own Stripe account on their side. You don't need to do anything beyond confirming the hire.

## Invoices

Every charge generates a Stripe invoice emailed to the account on file. You can also download them from the Customer Portal under **Billing history**.

## Pricing

| Plan | Scout cap | Chora fee | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 10 active scouts | 20% of scout fee | £0/mo |
| **Pro** | Unlimited scouts | 15% of scout fee | £99/mo |

Upgrade or downgrade from [**/hiring/billing**](https://app.trychora.com/hiring/billing). Downgrade is blocked if you have more than 10 active scouts. Deactivate some first.

## Troubleshooting failed charges

If Stripe fails to charge your card (expired, insufficient funds, 3DS block), the referral stays in **Hired** state, not Confirmed. Fix the payment method and click **Confirm hire** again. The scout isn't notified that the charge failed; they just see "waiting on hirer" until it goes through.

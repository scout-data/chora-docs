title: Invite scouts
description: Share an invite link so people can join your programme as scouts.
---

Chora programmes are **invite-only**. You share a link; anyone who signs in via that link joins your programme as a scout.

## Create an invite link

1. Go to [**/hiring/scouts**](https://trychora.com/hiring/scouts).
2. Click **Invite link** in the top-right.
3. Chora mints a fresh link like `trychora.com/join/<your-slug>/<token>`.
4. Copy it and share it anywhere: Slack, email, Lattice, your careers page.

<Note>
  The same link works for everyone. There's no per-person token. Trust comes from how you distribute it, not how Chora tracks it.
</Note>

## What scouts see

When a scout opens the link, they:

1. Land on your branded programme page (name, logo, tagline).
2. Click **Sign in with Google**. Chora creates their account automatically.
3. Land on a four-step walkthrough explaining how referrals and payouts work.
4. Reach their scout dashboard at `/scouts`.

## Internal vs external scouts

Chora auto-classifies scouts based on their sign-up email:

| Class | How it's detected | Default payout route |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | Email matches a verified employer domain | Payroll CSV export |
| **External** | Any other email | Stripe Connect (scout adds their own bank) |

See [Create your programme](/hirers/create-programme#verify-your-email-domain-optional-but-recommended) to verify a domain.

## Rotate or disable a link

On [**/hiring/scouts**](https://trychora.com/hiring/scouts), find the invite-links section:

- **Rotate.** Invalidates the current link and mints a new one. Scouts who've already joined stay in the programme.
- **Disable.** Stops accepting new joiners without replacing the URL.

## Capacity

Free programmes can hold up to **10 active scouts**. Once you hit the cap, the invite link shows a "programme full" page until you either deactivate a scout or upgrade to Pro.
